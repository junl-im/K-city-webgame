# CannonTale development rules

- Independent web game under `junl-im/K-city-webgame/CannonTale/`.
- Physical puzzle adventure and turn-based artillery duel are equally important.
- No SVG, emoji, external fonts, or copied third-party art. Use original illustrated bitmap assets.
- Keep desktop/mobile fixed-screen layout and no body scrolling.
- Browser Back: close open overlays first, return to the game menu if playing, and show a themed exit confirmation from the menu. Cancel preserves state; Leave navigates to the previous page or the AI playground hub on direct entry.
- Preserve saved progress across renamed display titles; do not break sibling games or their Pages paths.
